Positive and Negative Numbers, Powers and Roots

Prepared by: Aristotle D. Inigo

Positive and negative numbers, powers, and roots show up in budgets, stock, construction, and safety. Answer the questions below and then click Submit Quiz.

1. A project budget is currently –$2,000 (overspent). The team saves $500 this week. What is the new balance?

2. A freezer in a food warehouse goes from –18°C to –12°C. What is the temperature change?

3. A customer's bank account shows $35. They buy goods worth $20. What is the new balance?

4. A warehouse starts the day with 250 units. It ships 90 units and receives 40 units. Which expression shows the net change?

5. A metal rod must be 50 mm ± 0.3 mm. A rod measures 49.7 mm. What is its deviation from the target?

6. In an electrical formula, power is given by P = I²R. If the current I doubles, what happens to I²?

7. A right-angled corner of a construction site is 6 m by 8 m. What is the diagonal distance across the corner?

8. A storage tank’s volume is proportional to the cube of its side length. If the side length doubles, what happens to the volume?

9. A ladder leans against a wall. The foot is 9 m from the wall and the ladder reaches 12 m up. Approximately how long is the ladder?

10. A company invests $1,000 at 10% interest per year, compounded annually. After 2 years, the amount is 1000 × 1.1². What does the ² represent?
